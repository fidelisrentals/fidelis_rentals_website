About Us

Built on Trust &
Integrity

Fidelis Rentals was founded on a simple belief: that renting should be a comfortable, stress-free experience for everyone involved. Fidelis — Latin for trust — reflects our commitment to tenants and owners alike.

We're a small operation — that means you always know who you're dealing with, responses are fast, and every property gets real attention — not just a work order number.

Responsiveness

We answer calls and messages promptly. All tenants get the owner's personal cell number. Maintenance issues don't wait.

Transparency

Clear leases, fair pricing, and no hidden surprises — ever.

Quality Homes

We maintain our properties to a high standard before and during occupancy.

Community

The owner is a Missouri S&T Alumni from 2023.
